feat(P14-1): a real corpus pipeline, and 1075 knowledge cards behind it

Seven pure-Python scripts (tools/kb_fetch/) implement §2.3c's pipeline end to
end: sources.yaml -> fetch -> extract -> dedupe -> licence gate -> chunk ->
annotate -> import_cards. Two source kinds share one pipeline: public-domain
Project Gutenberg texts (mode: http, real url/author/licence/sha256) and
self-authored notes (mode: manual, licence OwnWork, sha256 recomputable).

Corpus: 943 generated cards + the 132 handwritten ones = 1075 across the nine
domains (>= 900 total, >= 60 per domain, >= 5% share). K1 source-ref
completeness, K2 licence compliance and K4 dedupe ratio are all 100%; the report
is committed at knowledge/cards_generated/import_report.json.

Findings that changed the implementation (each one measured, not guessed):

- SimHash by token frequency collapsed long English books onto one fingerprint
  (79 false near-duplicate pairs among 26 documents). Binary weighting over the
  token *set* takes that to 0 while still catching real near-duplicates.
- Uniform chunk sampling wasted most LLM calls: six of eight chunks from a 1912
  camera manual carried no shot-craft knowledge at all. Chunks are now picked
  per domain by an auditable keyword score, and notes are chunked by section.
- Model-authored keywords must not be part of the retrieval surface. They pushed
  generated cards ahead of the curated ones (comp_thirds lost to gen_cine_0050
  by 0.07; axis_cross fell to rank 10 for "越轴"), so generated cards are indexed
  by title+content only, and axis_cross's curated keyword list was widened.
- A unit test called validate_licenses.run() against the real output paths and
  silently overwrote work/licensed.jsonl with a single record; the pipeline tests
  now redirect every module-level output path through an autouse fixture.

block_director/knowledge/hypergraph.py is the hypergraph contract layer
(16 node kinds / 10 qualifiers / 6 edge types, the frozen edge_id formula, the
nine validation gates, dedup+merge, the four-step retrieval and atomic
rendering), with tests/test_hypergraph_schema.py covering every gate and both
directions of the build identity.

Not done yet, and not claimed: fixtures/knowledge_holdout.jsonl (>= 1200 entries
with per-entry human review). The generator, its swap-family annotator and the
six-gate checker are in place, but the set needs a local-model run plus the
human 10-15% review that agent annotation cannot stand in for.
