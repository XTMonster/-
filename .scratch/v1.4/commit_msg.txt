feat(P16): make the composition gate and the assertion hygiene provable

W10 (SS5 P16), first slice.

- cinema/composition.py: a subject that is (almost) entirely out of frame can no
  longer score well. Headroom, occlusion and balance all become vacuously
  satisfied when the frame holds no subject at all, so moving a character 30 m
  away still scored 60/100. The score is now capped at 20 whenever the primary
  subject's inside ratio is <= 5%, which is what makes RK-10's reverse mutation
  (break the framing -> the score must fall by >= 20) actually hold, and what
  stops a threshold of -1e9 from staying green.
- New Blender probe tools/blender_tests/test_composition_gate.py (RK-10):
  * the pass rate is computed with RK-10's own numerator/denominator (non-empty
    shots whose type is not ELS/LS; numerator = composition_score >=
    composition_threshold), never from AuditResult.passed;
  * the threshold is asserted to be the auditor's own 70.0 read independently;
  * the reverse mutation breaks ceil(0.3 * denominator) + 1 shots (at least
    two) and requires the rate to fall below 70% and every broken shot to lose
    at least 20 points. Measured: rate 1.00 -> 0.00 with drops of 50.0 / 62.8 /
    62.8.
  Registered in run_tests.ps1 (30 -> 31).
- New tools/check_assertions.py: a bpy-free static check for tautological
  assertions (assert True, assert 1 == 1, assert not False). It scans 118
  files and stays honest about what an empty assertion is: a bare
  ``assert findings`` is meaningful and is not flagged. tests/test_assertion_
  hygiene.py runs it over the repo and pins two negative/positive cases.
- New pure unit case in test_composition.py for the out-of-frame cap.

python -m pytest tests/test_composition.py -> 21 passed; the composition-gate,
